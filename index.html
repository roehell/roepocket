Before resetting Pocket Ledger

Download the reports you want to keep for each trip. A JSON backup preserves your editable records and is recommended even if you save reports. Reset deletes Pocket Ledger records from this browser, including receipts, trips, photos, and budgets. It cannot be undone without a backup.

🔐 Your data

Data and receipt images are stored in this browser, not uploaded to a server. Back up regularly: clearing Safari website data or changing devices may erase everything. Large photos may exceed browser storage limits.

Restoring replaces current data. Keep backup files somewhere safe.
